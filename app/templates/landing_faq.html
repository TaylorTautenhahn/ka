Getting started

Who is BidBoard for?

Fraternity and sorority recruitment teams. Members, officers, and chapter heads can keep profiles, notes, ratings, and follow-ups in one shared place.

How do I find my chapter?

Open the chapter directory, search for your organization, and choose its workspace. Use the account approved for that chapter. If it isn't listed, check with your chapter leadership or platform administrator.

Where can I see how it works?

Read the product tour for a closer look at profiles, coordination, and meeting prep.

Chapter access

Is our workspace separate from other chapters?

Each organization has a separate workspace, database, branding, and account approvals.

Does everyone have the same access?

No. Head, officer, and member roles have different permissions. What you can view or change depends on your role in that organization.

Can we use our chapter's branding?

Yes. Organization names, logos, and colors can be configured independently for each workspace.

Day to day

Can I use BidBoard on my phone?

Yes. Mobile views support searching profiles, adding ratings, and following up during recruitment events.

Can we bring in an existing recruitment list?

Yes. Import a CSV, including responses exported from Google Forms. The import flow includes validation and duplicate handling.

What can we prepare for a chapter meeting?

Build a shortlist and review meeting packets with profile details, conversation notes, and rating history.

Between seasons

Can we export and back up our data?

BidBoard includes CSV export bundles and database snapshots. Your administrator manages backups and storage through the admin tools.

What happens at the end of a season?

Season archive and reset tools let leadership preserve historical context and prepare the live board for the next recruitment cycle.

What keeps data available after an update?

The deployment needs correctly configured persistent storage. Startup checks and admin diagnostics help your administrator verify those settings; backups should be maintained separately.
